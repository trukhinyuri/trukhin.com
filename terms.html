agent-tools · last updated 26 August 2026

agent-tools is a personal automation tool. It is written, run and used by Yuri Trukhin on his own computers, to read his own accounts on services he already uses. It is not a product, it is not offered to anyone else, and there is no sign-up.

Who may use it

One person: the author. There are no other users, no accounts to create and no way for a third party to connect an account to it.

What it does

It reads data from services the author is already signed in to, through each service's own documented API and with credentials the author issued to himself. Everything it retrieves stays on his machines. It publishes nothing, posts nothing and sends nothing on anyone's behalf.

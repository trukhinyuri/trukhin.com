Privacy Policy

agent-tools · last updated 26 August 2026

agent-tools is a personal tool with exactly one user: its author. This page exists because the platforms it connects to require a privacy policy. Its short version is that no one else's data is involved, and nothing is sent anywhere.

What is collected

Nothing is collected from anyone. The tool reads the author's own data from services he is signed in to — his own profile, his own posts, his own messages and calendar — using credentials he issued to himself.

Where it goes

Onto the author's own computers, and nowhere else. There is no server, no database, no hosting and no backend. Nothing is sold, shared, or handed to a third party. There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Credentials

API tokens are stored locally with owner-only permissions and in the author's own password manager. They are never transmitted anywhere except to the service that issued them.

Retention and revocation

Retrieved data is kept locally for as long as it is useful and deleted with the machine's own files. Access can be revoked at any moment from the settings of the connected service, which immediately ends the tool's ability to read anything.

Children

The tool is not directed at anyone, children included, and has no users other than its author.
